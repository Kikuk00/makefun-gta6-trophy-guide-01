    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions: story first, then strangers, then collectibles, activities, and clean-up. The platinum unlocks when the other 54 are done.

      Missable trophies — 10 to watch

      Missable here means tied to a one-time story moment, a partner moment, or a single attempt that may not come back. Chapter and mission-completion trophies are not missable: finish the mission and they unlock, and a failed mission can always be retried. For the ten below, keep a save before you try them.

        Sources & honesty

        Hidden trophies

        PlayStation hides 28 of the 55 until they are earned. This guide marks as hidden the trophies whose descriptions are vague or story-bound — that is our reading of the list, and the guide says so wherever a hidden trophy appears.

        Offline & private

        This guide is plain files: it makes no network request at all. Your progress is saved only on your own machine (localStorage), and export/import moves it as a file you control. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere in this guide.
